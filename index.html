ABOUT MGESOL

Science-Driven

MGESOL is an environmental consulting firm specializing in water resources management. We combine rigorous scientific methods with modern cloud-based computing to deliver actionable insights for complex environmental challenges.

From hydrologic investigations to integrated water resources management, our multidisciplinary team brings expertise in groundwater, surface water, agricultural systems, and geospatial analysis to every project.

COMMUNITY MISSION

Giving Back Through
Research

We believe environmental science should be accessible — not gatekept by budget constraints. Our Low Budget High Impact Research initiative channels our expertise toward communities and organizations that need it most.

LBHIR delivers peer-quality research at a fraction of conventional cost, making rigorous environmental data available to rural municipalities, conservation districts, and nonprofits across the world.

Low Budget · High Impact · Research Science in service of the public good — accessible, rigorous, and openly shared where possible.

● Accessible Environmental Science

Delivering rigorous, publication-quality research at lower cost — removing financial barriers that prevent rural communities from accessing critical environmental data.

● Applied Research Partnerships

Collaborating with universities, tribal nations, and conservation districts to co-develop studies addressing real-world water and land management challenges with implementable outcomes.

● Open Data Commitment

Where feasible, LBHIR research outputs are made publicly available — contributing to growing environmental knowledge and future scientific inquiry.

● Community Capacity Building

Training local stakeholders to interpret and use environmental data — empowering communities to make informed long-term decisions.
